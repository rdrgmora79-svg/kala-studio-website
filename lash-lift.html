Things people ask us

Good questions
deserve real answers.

What is a lash lift exactly?

A lash lift is a semi-permanent treatment that curls your natural lashes upward from the root — similar to a perm, but for your lashes. The result is eyes that look wider and more awake, with lashes that appear longer and fuller, all without extensions or daily curling.

How is a lash lift different from lash extensions?

Lash extensions add individual synthetic lashes to yours — they look dramatic but require fills every 2–3 weeks and careful maintenance. A lash lift works with your natural lashes only — no added hair, no glue, no infills. It's the low-maintenance option for clients who want results without the upkeep.

How long does a lash lift last?

Results typically last 6 to 8 weeks — the full growth cycle of your natural lashes. After that, your lashes will gradually return to their natural position as new growth comes in.

Does it damage my natural lashes?

When performed correctly by a trained artist, a lash lift does not damage your natural lashes. At Kala Studio we use professional-grade solutions and careful technique to ensure your lash health is protected throughout the treatment.

Can I wear mascara after a lash lift?

Yes — after 24 hours you can wear mascara normally. Most of our clients find they need much less (or none at all) after their lift and tint, since the lashes already look dark and defined.

How do I prepare for my lash lift appointment?

Arrive with clean lashes — no mascara, no lash serum, and no lash curling that day. Remove contact lenses before the appointment. Avoid waterproof mascara for 24 hours after the service to protect the curl while it sets.
